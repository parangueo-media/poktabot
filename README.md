# POKTABOT
POKTABOT is an educational robotic game inspired in the ancient mesoamerican game known as Juego de pelota. Many varieties of the game are still being played Today.

![POKTABOT Cancha del juego](https://github.com/parangueo-media/poktabot/blob/main/images/basic_v1.0/POKTABOT_CANCHA_COMPLETA__2590.jpg)

POKTABOT is created and maintained by Ondrej Michalak and Laura Rodriguez from Parangueo Media s.r.o., more info, events and news can be found at https://www.parangueo.com/poktabot/ .

POKTABOT is to be played at robotic and cultural events as a competition for kids, teenagers or adults in three different levels:

**BASIC** – (6-12yo) Playing with standartized robots and joysticks provided by the event organiser. Recommended and suitable platforms are micro:bit and Cutebot or similar.

**HACKER** – (13-18yo) High-School level. Players brings their own programmable joysticks with programmable buttons and programmable robots in size 10x10x10 cm max. Recommended and suitable platforms are micro:bit and Cutebot or similar. Platform such as Raspberry PI or Arduino may be considered for elder teenagers.

**ROBOT** – (16+ yo) University and adult level. Players bring their own fully autonomous robots.


# POKTABOT RULES for THE BASIC LEVEL v1.0

POKTABOT is a game for two players. Each player controls one robot.

## Goal of the Game
Push the ball with your robot and make it pass through the opponent’s ring.

## Equipment

One playing field (large table size or floor)

2 robots (approximately 10 × 10 × 10 cm)

1 ball (the size of a golf ball)

2 rings (inside diameter: 5 cm)

2 joysticks to control the robots

## Setup

Place each robot at its starting position.
Put the ball in the center of the field.
Players take their joysticks.

## How to Play

When the start signal sounds, players move their robots.
The robots compete for the ball.
Each player tries to push the ball through the opponent’s ring.

## End of the Game

The first player to push the ball through the opponent’s ring wins.
If nobody scores within 3 minutes, the game ends with no winner.

## Notes

Robots may have decorative parts extending beyond the size limit, but these parts cannot be active or functional.
